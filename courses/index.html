FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a technical background?

No. GRC rewards analytical thinking, clear writing, and comfort talking to people across a business. Technical knowledge helps but is not the entry barrier. Lesson 1 of the free course explains why.

What will I be able to do after the free mini-course?

You will understand what GRC is, know the frameworks by name and purpose, and be able to make an informed decision about whether to pursue the field. It will not make you job-ready; the full program does that.

What does the paid program include that the free one does not?

The paid program adds the four practical labs (audit, risk assessment, policy writing, awareness campaign), the job hunting module, per-module quizzes, resume bullets, and every template and workbook used in the labs.

Is there a certificate?

The paid program includes a completion certificate you can add to your portfolio. It is not a substitute for vendor certifications like CISA or CISM, and we do not claim otherwise.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If the paid program is not for you, email hi@zabez.com within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. No questions, no hoops.
