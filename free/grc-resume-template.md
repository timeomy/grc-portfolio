### ATS rules that get you past filters
1. **One page.** No objective paragraph, no photo.
2. Mirror the JD's exact keywords (scanners check literally).
3. Bold results, not job titles or responsibilities.
4. No tables/graphics for text-heavy ATS; simple single column.
5. Dates as `Mon YYYY` so ATS parses them.
